      07

      FAQ

      What is a main, and what is a beta?

      The main is the runner: the coin a narrative grew from. Betas are the coins riding it, named after it or built around it. Most betas don't actually follow their main. TEAM makes them.

      How does TEAM find betas?

      It takes the main's ticker and name and searches Jupiter's token index, Dexscreener's pairs and every pump.fun launch it reads straight from Solana in the last three days. For the biggest betas it measures follow: how closely their 15-minute candles move with the main's.

      What does the maker actually do?

      Every pass it measures each beta against the main since kickoff. When a beta falls a full band behind, it buys it. When a beta runs a full band ahead, it trims it. Once per step, never twice for the same step. The main is never traded.

      Who holds the money?

      The maker wallet is created and held by the server so it can sign swaps while you sleep. Only your wallet can change its rules, pause it or withdraw, and withdrawals only ever go to your wallet. Its key is sealed with AES-256-GCM. That is custody: fund only what you can lose.

      Can I lose money?

      Yes. Buying a beta that's behind is a bet that it catches up. Some betas are behind because they're dying. Meme coins can go to zero. The lab replay shows how the rule behaved in the past, not what happens next.

      What does it cost?

      Nothing on top. The maker pays normal network fees and Jupiter's route on each swap. Some SOL is kept back in the maker for fees and token-account rent.

      How do I get my SOL back?

      Open your team and press withdraw. The next passes sell every beta, close the empty token accounts and send every remaining lamport to your wallet.
